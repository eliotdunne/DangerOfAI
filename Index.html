The Dangers of Artificial Intelligence

By Sam, Luca & Eliot

Introduction

Many people are convinced that AI is safe and not a threat, but this could not be farther from the truth. Not only is it a major threat to people jobs but there have also been incidents of AI going rouge. AI is also known for spreading false information which spreads misinformation. Some AI models are also known for being very biased and even dangerous. The most common dangers of AI include:

Misinformation

Many AI can make Ultra-realistic videos that perfectly replicate real news and media for someone not familiar with it. This can spread misinformation. It can be used to hack or exploit cybersecurity tools and destroy software infrastructure.

AI Security

Many companies use AI security tools such as facial recognition and surveillance. These are usually cheaper because of the fact you do not have to pay for people to do it, but are much less accurate.

Environmental impact

Training AI is extremely bad for the environment as it drains electricity and water. This is very bad for the local community surrounding these massive data centres.

Economic Effects

AI takes away many tech jobs due to the fact that it is cheaper and that companies can use it whenever they want as they want workers who don’t have good holidays or security. AI solves these problems for the company but at the expense of it being less reliable, trustworthy and the fact that the average person loses the ability to support themselves and their families.
